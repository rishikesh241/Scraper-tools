Question 2 of 375

You are an ML engineer at a global shoe store. You manage the ML models for the company's website. You are asked to build a model that will recommend new products to the user based on their purchase behavior and similarity with other users. What should you do?

Answer choices
Show answerHide answer

Correct answer: C

To recommend new products to users based on their purchase behavior and similarity with other users, the best approach is to build a collaborative filtering model. Collaborative filtering leverages user behavior data to find similarities between users and recommend items that similar users have liked or purchased. This method is well-suited for situations where you want to personalize recommendations based on user interactions and preferences. Classification and regression models are more common in other types of predictive modeling tasks, and knowledge-based filtering relies on explicit knowledge rather than user interaction patterns, making them less appropriate for this use case.

Question 3 of 375

Your team trained and tested a DNN regression model with good results. Six months after deployment, the model is performing poorly due to a change in the distribution of the input data. How should you address the input differences in production?

Answer choices
Show answerHide answer

Correct answer: A

When a deployed model starts performing poorly due to a change in the distribution of the input data, the appropriate step is to create alerts to monitor for data skew and retrain the model. This approach ensures that the model adapts to new patterns in the data, maintaining its accuracy and effectiveness. Feature selection or hyperparameter tuning, as mentioned in other options, are not directly addressing the root issue, which is the change in data distribution. Continuous monitoring and timely retraining are essential for handling such changes effectively.

Question 4 of 375

You are working on a Neural Network-based project. The dataset provided to you has columns with different ranges. While preparing the data for model training, you discover that gradient optimization is having difficulty moving weights to a good solution. What should you do?

Answer choices
Show answerHide answer

Correct answer: B

When working with a neural network and encountering difficulties in gradient optimization, especially in the presence of features with different ranges, the appropriate step is to normalize the data. Normalization scales the dataset's numeric fields to a common range, typically between 0 and 1. This process ensures that features contribute equally to the gradient descent optimization, facilitating better and faster convergence towards an optimal solution. Therefore, using the representation transformation (normalization) technique is the appropriate approach in this scenario.

Question 5 of 375

You have deployed a model on Vertex AI for real-time inference. During an online prediction request, you get an “Out of Memory” error. What should you do?

Answer choices
Show answerHide answer

Correct answer: B

During an 'Out of Memory' error in an online prediction request, the error suggests that the data being sent in each request is too large and exceeds the available memory. To address this, sending the request again with a smaller batch of instances can reduce the amount of data processed at a time, potentially avoiding the out-of-memory error and successfully completing the prediction request.
