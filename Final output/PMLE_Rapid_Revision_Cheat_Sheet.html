Q#12 Sec 4 C 1 = AI Platform, 2 = AI Platform, 3 = Cloud Natural Language API. Use custom models on AI Platform/Vertex AI for proprietary ticket priority & resolution time, and pre-trained Cloud Natural Language API for general sentiment analysis.

Q#23 Sec 2 A 1 = Dataflow, 2 = BigQuery. Regional ETL + PII redaction on 1M+ daily files uses Cloud Dataflow, landing into regional BigQuery for ANSI SQL analytics and BI dashboards.
Q#53 Sec 3 B Group split by author (Train: [TextA1, TextA2, TextD1, TextD2], Test: [TextB1, TextB2], Eval: [TextC1, TextC2]). Never put texts from the same author across train/test/eval when predicting author attributes, or the model memorizes author identity (data leakage).
Q#126 Sec 6 D TPU Profiler High Infeed % (D): When the TPU spends most of its step time waiting on input (infeed), optimize tf.data with parallel reads (interleave), parallel processing (num_parallel_calls), and prefetch.
